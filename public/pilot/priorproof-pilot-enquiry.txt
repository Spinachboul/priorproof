PriorProof hospital desk pilot enquiry brief

Purpose
Evaluate whether evidence-linked preparation helps a hospital insurance desk complete cashless pre-authorization drafts with less staff time and acceptable review effort.

Share this brief with the hospital staff member coordinating an internal pilot review. The site does not collect enquiries online. Please omit patient names, documents, identifiers and other case-level information.

Discussion prompts
- Hospital or desk type, workflow owner and approximate daily case volume.
- Steps and tools used today for document collection, verification and request preparation.
- Which handoffs or clarification loops the desk wants to measure.
- Proposed pilot period, number of synthetic scenarios and staff reviewers.
- Local inference hardware, internal approval, access roles, secure deployment and retention requirements.

Suggested measurement
- Compare manual and assisted time from intake to an approved draft. Include verification, corrections and sign-off in both runs.
- Compare checklist completeness, evidence accuracy, missed conflicts, unsupported output and unresolved items.
- Record rework and clarification counts separately. Track financial and insurer outcomes only if independently observable and properly authorized.
- Record local inference, maintenance and staff costs separately from any estimated value of recovered capacity.

Before live records
The current prototype is for fictional records. A live workflow would require hospital approval, security and privacy assessment, an appropriate legal basis and notices, organization-level roles, secure infrastructure, and a defined retention and incident-response process. Insurers retain all authorization decisions.
