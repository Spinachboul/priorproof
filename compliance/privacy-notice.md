# Privacy notice PP-NOTICE-2026-11-v1

PriorProof is a fictional demonstration for staff preparing evidence-linked hospital pre-authorization drafts. Upload fictional records exclusively. Use the case form to record the stated purpose, data categories, intended recipients and staff-selected processing basis. Staff confirmation records an administrative action. Actual patient consent and other legal bases require a separate organizational process and supporting evidence.

The backend stores case details, original uploads, extracted text, derived evidence, review decisions and activity in SQLite and local files on the device hosting the API. Deterministic screening masks detected identifier values in a separate text preview. Optional Ollama extraction sends limited labelled source lines to the configured loopback service and excludes lines containing detected identifier patterns. Browser and phone clients send records to the configured backend. A LAN HTTP connection does not encrypt that traffic.

The shared fictional demo account can read, review, export and delete its authorized cases. A named insurer or TPA is an intended recipient recorded by staff. The app does not transmit a request to an insurer. Staff can configure a case review date and record a legal hold. Review dates do not automatically delete records. Downloaded copies and backups require separate handling.

Contact the operator who provided the demo to ask a privacy question. Signed-in staff can record access, correction, erasure and grievance requests in Case privacy and confidentiality. A register entry records administrative handling. The operator must verify identity and address any applicable rights through the organization’s approved channel.
