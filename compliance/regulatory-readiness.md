# DPDP reference mapping

The Trust Centre presents implementation status and outstanding organizational work for the fictional prototype. It does not make a legal compliance determination.

## Official references and commencement

- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/1/a2023-22.pdf) and [Act commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_0_00003_2023-22_1763464807080&filename=c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf&type=hindinotification).
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) and [MeitY publication and corrigendum](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa).

The commencement notifications are dated 13 November 2025. Sections 5, 7, 8 and 11–13, section 6(1)–(8) and 6(10), and Rules 3, 6, 7 and 14 are scheduled for 13 May 2027 through their eighteen-month provisions. Section 6(9), concerning Consent Managers, commenced separately on 13 November 2026. This mapping was checked on 28 November 2026.
